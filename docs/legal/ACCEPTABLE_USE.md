# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective:** October 4, 2026
**Provider:** M. Arslan (independent developer)

This policy explains, in plain English, what Ghosthread AI is for and
what it refuses to do. It exists so that users, reviewers, and any
platform that hosts or distributes the Software can see the project's
boundaries clearly.

The one-sentence version:

> **Ghosthread AI is a productivity tool for legitimate desktop
> automation on computers you own or are authorized to use. It will
> refuse to operate in environments where doing so would violate
> laws, third-party terms of service, or common-sense ethical
> boundaries.**

---

## What Ghosthread is for

- Automating repetitive work on your own machine.
- Organizing your own files and folders.
- Reading your own screen for errors, code, and text.
- Managing your own notifications, media, and system settings.
- Handling your own messages and communications.
- Diagnosing problems in your own development environment.
- Any task where you have the legal and contractual right to
  automate.

## What Ghosthread refuses to do

### 1. Games and anti-cheat

The Software detects game processes and anti-cheat software and
**refuses to operate alongside them.** This is not a suggestion. The
software pauses or declines to launch when these environments are
detected.

Attempting to bypass this restriction is a violation of the Terms of
Service and will result in immediate loss of access.

*Why: this protects you from account bans, protects game developers
from unfair play, and protects the project from legal liability. It is
non-negotiable.*

### 2. Banking, payments, and financial systems

The Software **will not automate banking portals, payment processors,
brokerage accounts, or cryptocurrency exchanges** where such
automation is prohibited by the platform's terms. Even where it might
be permitted, it's strongly discouraged.

Financial messages and credential requests are flagged and escalated
to you rather than acted on.

### 3. Systems you don't own

You may not use Ghosthread to automate access to any system, account,
or resource you don't own or have explicit written authorization to
access.

### 4. Harmful, illegal, or deceptive activity

You may not use Ghosthread for harassment, spam, fraud, phishing,
malware distribution, bypassing security controls, violating privacy
laws, or any activity that harms other people or violates applicable
law.

### 5. Circumventing DRM or access controls

You may not use Ghosthread to bypass digital rights management,
license checks, or any protection mechanism on software, media, or
services you don't have the rights to access.

### 6. Reverse-engineering Ghosthread itself

You may not decompile, reverse-engineer, extract source code from, or
repackage Ghosthread AI without explicit written permission.

## Third-party terms of service

Many applications and services have their own terms that restrict or
prohibit automation. Some messaging platforms, social networks,
financial services, and marketplaces do.

**You are responsible** for reviewing and complying with the terms of
every application and service you automate with Ghosthread. I don't
monitor, enforce, or take responsibility for your compliance with
third-party terms.

If you're unsure whether a specific use case is permitted, contact the
platform directly before automating.

## What happens if you violate this policy

- Access may be suspended or terminated immediately, without refund.
- Actions may be reported to relevant authorities where required by
  law.
- You may be held personally liable for any harm caused by your
  violation.

## Good-faith ambiguity

If you have a legitimate use case that falls into a gray area — for
example, automating a piece of software whose terms you're unsure
about — **reach out before using Ghosthread for it.** I'll try to help
you figure out a safe path.
